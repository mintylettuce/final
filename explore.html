Use the dropdown to focus on one county or compare all three. Hover over a point for its block group and estimates; drag across the chart to zoom, then use the plot controls to zoom in or out.

Interactive income and age

Each point is one of 3,589 block groups. The estimates describe the block group as a whole; they are not measurements of individual residents.

Compare county distributions

These boxplots summarize the distribution of block-group median income and median age in each county. A higher box means higher estimates for a typical block group; wide boxes and whiskers show variation within a county.

Data source: U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey, 2020–2024 five-year estimates.
